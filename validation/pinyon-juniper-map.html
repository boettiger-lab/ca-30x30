Our pinyon-juniper layer includes 335,000 acres of Nevada

CA 30×30 Explorer · validation against the 2025 Biodiversity Assessment
This was our defect, not a disagreement. Our FVEG-derived habitat layer is not clipped to California. 19.4% of its pinyon-juniper — 335,544 ac — lies in Nevada. Nevada habitat cannot intersect a California conservation unit, so all of it scored as non-conserved and pulled our percentage down.

Turn on Non-conserved pinyon-juniper and follow the band northeast: it runs straight across the state line past Yerington and Hawthorne. That is not California habitat outside conservation units — it is Nevada.

Clipped to California we reproduce the Assessment to 0.6 pp — the same discretization noise as every other habitat class.

Juniper was the control that misled us. Juniper (whrnum=26) is 99.9% in-state, so it reproduced the Assessment all along. We read that as proof the pipeline was sound — it was. The extent was not, and only the classes with out-of-state coverage diverged. Switch to the juniper layer: almost nothing east of the line.
