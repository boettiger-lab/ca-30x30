30x30 Biodiversity Assessment Geospatial Companion Tool

Welcome to the 30x30 Biodiversity Assessment Geospatial Companion Tool. This tool is designed to explore and analyze conservation status and distribution of habitats and other important ecological features across the state to enhance knowledge of biodiversity conservation in support of 30x30. It is intended to be a companion tool to the California Biodiversity Network's (CBN's) 30x30 Biodiversity Assessment and draws upon datasets vetted by the CBN Biodiversity Assessment working group. It is currently based upon the 2025 Conservation Area data.

LLM interface tools are extremely difficult to comprehensively test (see proposed tool benchmarks).
